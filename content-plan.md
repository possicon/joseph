### Personal Introduction
Hello! I'm Joseph Peters, a passionate engineer and innovator dedicated to building technology that serves humanity's greatest needs. As a student committed to expanding access to education and empowering underrepresented communities, I bring together technical expertise with ethical reasoning grounded in my studies of Justice and Biblical Theology.

Through my work on award-winning projects like DiscoverU (2025 HackBack Winner), robotics competitions, and community service initiatives like Giveback Backpacks, I have consistently demonstrated that technology can be a powerful force for social good. My approach is analytical and persistent—I break down complex problems into solvable parts and push through challenges until meaningful solutions emerge.

Currently, I am conducting independent research on the gamification of education and its impact on student motivation. I earned a Grade A in Johns Hopkins University's Engineering Innovation Program and have raised over $50,000 for the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society. I love taking apart and reassembling systems—whether robotics, automotive parts, or software—because understanding how things work is the first step to making them better.

### Core Values

**Innovation**
To me, innovation means identifying problems that others overlook and creating solutions that make a real difference. It's about pushing boundaries and challenging the status quo. Throughout my journey, I've consistently sought to build technology that doesn't just work—it empowers. From developing DiscoverU to help students discover their career paths, to designing an assistive robotic arm for people with disabilities, innovation drives every project I undertake. This mindset has opened doors to opportunities and has shaped who I am today.

**Integrity**
For me, integrity is about connecting technical innovation with ethical and moral reasoning. It's about asking not just "Can we build this?" but "Should we build this?" and "Who will it serve?" This value is deeply rooted in my studies of Justice and Biblical Theology. I believe that technology should be designed with ethics, accessibility, and equity at the core. Every line of code I write and every system I design must reflect a commitment to doing what's right, not just what's possible.

**Perseverance**
To me, perseverance is what fuels my ability to tackle complex challenges and see them through to completion. It's the driving force that keeps me going when projects get difficult. Whether it's debugging code at 2 AM, redesigning a robot component for the fifth time, or conducting rigorous research studies, I'm willing to go to great lengths to achieve meaningful results. This persistence has been crucial in every achievement—from winning HackBack to raising $50,000 for cancer research.

**Innovator**
I believe being an innovator is about identifying pain points and creating solutions that expand access and opportunity. Through projects like DiscoverU, which addresses the career counseling gap affecting thousands of students, and my assistive robotics work, which makes independence more accessible, I've demonstrated how innovation can create real social impact. As someone who has benefited from mentors who believed in me, I understand the transformative power of encouragement and knowledge-sharing. ## Philosophy Page

In a world enriched by rapid technological advancement, ensuring that innovation serves everyone—not just the privileged few—is essential. In every project I build, I constantly reflect on who benefits and who might be left behind. Whose voices are represented in the design? If they aren't being heard, how can I help change that?

My commitment to ethical technology and educational equity fuels my passion for building solutions that empower underrepresented communities and expand access to opportunity. I refuse to accept that innovation should be a privilege. Technology has the power to level the playing field, but only if we design it with ethics, accessibility, and equity at the core.

Through my experiences with DiscoverU, assistive robotics, and community-driven innovation, I have seen firsthand how thoughtful design can transform lives. ## Innovation Page

### Featured Innovations

**DiscoverU - 2025 HackBack Winner**
An innovative, gamified, AI-supported career counseling platform that helps high school students discover meaningful career paths aligned with their passions and strengths. Through rigorous research involving 60+ students across three public schools, DiscoverU demonstrated significantly higher satisfaction, increased confidence, and greater career exploration compared to traditional counseling methods.

**Assistive Robotic Arm**
A 4-degree-of-freedom robotic arm designed to help people with limited mobility complete essential daily tasks. Built with Arduino, servo motors, and 3D-printed components, this open-source prototype demonstrates how affordable robotics can increase independence and improve quality of life.

**Robotics Competition Leadership**
As Programming and Design Lead, I develop competition-ready robots from concept through execution, utilizing CAD design, Java/Python programming, and strategic problem-solving to compete successfully in multiple tournaments.
